FAQ

Questions companies ask us

What is the minimum order quantity?

Custom branding makes sense from 25 boxes upward. Below that we can still do custom greeting cards with ready packaging — ask us.

How far in advance should we order?

For standard packages with logo printing, 7–10 days is comfortable. For fully custom box designs, allow 2–3 weeks. Festival peaks (Diwali especially) fill up early — book ahead.

Can we personalise each box with the employee's name?

Yes — names can appear on the greeting card, a gift tag, or the box itself. You share the list, we handle the rest.

Is the packaging vegetarian / any allergen info?

All items are vegetarian. Nuts and dry fruits are packed in dedicated compartments; chocolates list full ingredients. Tell us about allergies and we'll suggest safe swaps.

Can employees choose their own contents?

For smaller counts we keep boxes uniform. For larger orders we can run a preference round — e.g. a nut-only option for those who avoid sweets — with a simple form.

Do you deliver to individual employee addresses?

Yes — bulk delivery to the office is standard; individual courier delivery can be quoted for remote teams, including a personalised note per box.
